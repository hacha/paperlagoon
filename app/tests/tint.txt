# the world's colour (the values', the curves', on every page): it follows the music, not the knob. Through a crossing
# it is between the world the music is in and the one chosen, never there at once; it is there some seconds after the
# music is. tint: the colour as the screen has it (5-6-5 bits); tintr: its red (0..31)
wait 600
expect world 0
expect here 0
expect tint 38687
# to the horizon (a little violet: its red 22, the shallows' 18)
turn ALGO 1
wait 100
expect world 1
expect here 0
expect tintr <20
wait 900
expect tintr >18
expect tintr <22
wait 9000
expect crossing 0
expect here 1
wait 8000
expect tint 46399
# the same on every page
tap ENV
wait 300
expect tint 46399
tap HOME
# chosen and chosen back (called off, or there and back: as the phrase falls): the colour back with the music
turn ALGO 1
turn ALGO -1
wait 16000
expect crossing 0
expect here 1
expect tint 46399
# at rest there is no crossing: the world at once, its colour in some seconds, not at once
tap PLAY
wait 300
expect playing 0
turn ALGO 4
wait 100
expect world 5
expect here 5
expect tintr >22
expect tintr <31
wait 8000
expect tintr 31
