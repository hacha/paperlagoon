# the worlds' ladders: each world takes its scale from its own five (lagoon.c LADDER), so the worlds differ in harmony
# too. Told here by the notes to the octave: the letter's scales all have five, the moss's six or seven (the
# hexachords, lydian), the stair's seven (the modes: a step of the stair is a step of a scale); the horizon and the
# attic never seven
wait 600
expect world 0
expect scalelen <8
expect scalelen >4
turn ALGO 2
wait 9000
expect alg 3
expect scalelen 5
wait 60000
expect scalelen 5
turn ALGO 2
wait 9000
expect alg 2
expect scalelen >5
wait 60000
expect scalelen >5
turn ALGO 2
wait 9000
expect alg 6
expect scalelen 7
wait 90000
expect scalelen 7
turn ALGO -1
wait 9000
expect alg 5
expect scalelen <7
turn ALGO -4
wait 9000
expect alg 1
expect scalelen <7
